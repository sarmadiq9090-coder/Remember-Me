Remember Me: privacy and license

PRIVACY

Remember Me will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

- Tasks, notes, attachments and settings are stored only on your device. Remember Me has no account, analytics or telemetry.
- Backups are copied only to folders you choose. For a Google Drive folder, Google's own Drive app does the syncing, under Google's privacy policy.
- Links you click, such as the Google Drive download page, open in your default browser.
- Report a problem only opens a pre-filled GitHub issue in your browser, and only when you choose it. You can read and edit it, and nothing is sent unless you submit it. It contains the app and Windows versions, language, theme and recent log lines with paths removed, never your tasks, notes or file names.
- If you allow automatic updates, or choose Check now, Remember Me contacts GitHub Releases for this repository to check for and download new versions. That's a standard HTTPS request, covered by GitHub's privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), and none of your data is included. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Updates.

Uninstalling Remember Me keeps your data in %APPDATA%\Remember Me.
