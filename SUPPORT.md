# Support

## Questions and ideas

Ask how something works, suggest an idea or share how you use Remember Me in [Discussions](https://github.com/sarmadiq9090-coder/Remember-Me/discussions). Search there and in [Issues](https://github.com/sarmadiq9090-coder/Remember-Me/issues) first: your question may already have an answer.

## Problems

Open an issue with the [bug report form](https://github.com/sarmadiq9090-coder/Remember-Me/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include:

- the app version and Windows version: **Settings → About → Copy diagnostic info** copies both, plus the language and theme, and nothing from your tasks or notes;
- the steps that make the problem happen, and what you expected instead.

If it helps, attach the local log from **Settings → About → Open log folder**. It records events, counts and error codes only, never the text of your tasks, notes or file names.

Use the [feature request form](https://github.com/sarmadiq9090-coder/Remember-Me/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) for a specific improvement, and the [translation review form](https://github.com/sarmadiq9090-coder/Remember-Me/issues/new?template=translation.yml) for wording in your language.

## Keep your data private

Do not post your reminders, notes, attachments, backups or account details in public issues or discussions. If a screenshot shows personal text, cover it first.

Report security vulnerabilities privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
